I came to this from philosophy of science, which is a slightly awkward place to
run a course with this much biology in it. The reason it works is that the
questions turn out to be the same ones. A desert ant integrating its own stride
count has no independent reference against which to check the result, and
neither does a research programme checking its own instruments.

I convene the course and teach weeks 1, 11 and 12. If you disagree with
something in a lecture, say so in the seminar; the weeks where students have
argued me out of a position are the ones I have rewritten most.

## What to ask me

Assessment, extensions, and any case where you think a mark is wrong. Also
anything about the evidence grades — if you think a claim on this site is graded
wrongly, I would rather hear it than not, and it has happened.
