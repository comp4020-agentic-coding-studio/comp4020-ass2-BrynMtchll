I work on capacities that evolved more than once, which is the most useful
evidence there is that a capacity solves a real problem rather than being an
accident of one lineage. Bees, spiders and cephalopods arrived at parts of this
independently, and the fact that they did is why weeks 8 to 10 exist.

I teach weeks 2, 3, 4, 8, 9 and 10, and I mark the cognitive profiles.

## What to ask me

Which organism to profile. Come early, and come with two options rather than
one — I will usually tell you which has the literature to support the sections
you have to fill in. Charismatic animals are over-chosen and under-evidenced,
and the octopus is the worst offender.
