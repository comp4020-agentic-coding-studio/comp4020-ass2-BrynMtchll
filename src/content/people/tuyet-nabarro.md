My research is on predictive coding in sensory cortex, which means I spend most
of my time on the gap between what arrives at a sense organ and what a system
does with it. That gap is where this course lives.

I teach weeks 5, 6 and 7. Week 6 is the one people find most destabilising, and
I would rather you were destabilised in the seminar than three weeks later in
an assessment.

## What to ask me

The quizzes, especially the certainty scale — most students misuse it for the
first fortnight and then it clicks. Also anything about reading a methods
section, which is the skill the course assumes and does not teach directly.
