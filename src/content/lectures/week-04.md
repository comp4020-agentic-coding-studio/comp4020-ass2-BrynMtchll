Associative learning has access to co-occurrence and no access to causal structure. Superstition follows from the mechanism rather than indicating a fault in it — Skinner's 1948 pigeons are the demonstration, and from the pigeon's position the routine was working.

The second half is Pfungst on Clever Hans (1911), which established that the experimenter belongs to the apparatus. Every method the rest of the course relies on descends from that finding, including the blinding that decided the pea plant dispute in week 3.
