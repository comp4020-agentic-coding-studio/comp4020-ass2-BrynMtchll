This assessment runs in two parts, ten weeks apart.

## Part one — the position statement, end of week 3

Around 500 words on what you currently think intelligence is and where you think
it starts. No reading. No citations. No research.

Write it before you do any of the week 2 reading, and do not go back to it.

It is not marked. Submitting it is a **hurdle**: without it you cannot submit the
revision, and the revision is the assessment. It also cannot be extended past
the end of week 3, because a statement written in week 8 has already been
contaminated by the thing it is supposed to predate.

Most of these will be wrong in interesting ways. That is the design, not a
failure of the cohort. You are recording your own prior before the evidence
arrives, so that in ten weeks there is something to measure against.

## Part two — the revision, week 11

Three documents:

1. the original, unaltered
2. a revised version
3. a **memo** of roughly 1200 words

The memo is what is marked. The revised statement itself is not graded on its
quality, which matters — otherwise anyone who happened to be right in week 3
would be penalised, and anyone who wrote deliberately badly would be rewarded.

The memo answers three questions:

- **What did you change, and what specifically changed it?** Name the paper, the
  week or the argument. "I understand more now" is not an answer.
- **What did you keep, under pressure to change it?** This is the question that
  separates a revision from a capitulation.
- **What is still weakest in the revised version?**

## How this is marked, plainly

Both over-revision and under-revision fail.

Changing nothing means the course did not reach you. Changing everything means
you never held any of it in the first place — a memo that abandons every claim
is telling me there was no position to revise. What is being assessed is the
**shape** of the revision, not its extent.

Question three is where the marks concentrate. A revised statement presented as
now-correct has missed the entire course. One that says clearly what remains
unsupported is doing, on itself, what week 12 does to the field.
