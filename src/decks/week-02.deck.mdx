## That is a measurement and a comparison

And it is enough to solve a real problem.

Keep it as the floor. Everything later is added to **this**.

---

## Careful with your verbs

"Seeks", "wants", "searches for" — all projection.

Ask what is *held* in the system. The answer here is: a recent value, and a
direction of change.

# Now the useful animal

---

## What you have heard

The mantis shrimp sees millions more colours than you do.

Twelve photoreceptor types. Against our three.

300–720 nm.

---

## The inference is reasonable

More channels → finer discrimination.

That is how it works in us.

---

## Thoen et al., 2014

Behavioural wavelength discrimination, *Haptosquilla trispinosa*.

**Roughly ten times worse** than goldfish, butterflies, birds, or us.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

The popular claim does not overstate the finding. It inverts it.

---

## The proposal

Not comparing channels to compute a hue.

Reading **which of twelve narrow bins fires hardest**.

Recognition, not discrimination. Closer to a barcode scanner than an eye.

---

## Why build that

| Requirement | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Identify prey vs mate in milliseconds | speed over precision |
| Strike is committed and irreversible | *that*, not *which* |

Still unresolved as a mechanism. A 2021 review says so.

**[C]**

---

## The lesson for weeks 3 to 7

More sensors is not better sensing.

A sensory system is a **commitment** about which distinctions are worth paying
for.
