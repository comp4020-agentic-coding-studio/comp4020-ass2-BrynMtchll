# Reverse it

---

## The proposal

The system **generates** what it expects.

Only the mismatch travels up.

Clark, 2013. Friston, 2010.

---

## Why this took over

Illusions stop being a list of curiosities.

They become **predictions of the framework**.

---

## The checker-shadow

You understand exactly how it works.

You go on seeing it wrongly.

The inference runs below the level at which understanding operates.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

Nothing tags which parts of your experience were sensed and which were generated.

---

## Both arrive as perception

There is no marker.

No sense of construction.

No way in, from in here.

# Now the objection

---

## The dark room problem

If a system minimises prediction error —

why not sit in a featureless room and never move?

---

## The standard reply

Expected free energy. Novelty-seeking. Epistemic value.

**[C]** — and you should be able to say why that reply is contested.

---

## Sharper still

Litwin & Miłkowski — *unification by fiat*

A framework that accommodates every result is in an awkward position.

Saying **what would move it** is this week's work.

---

## Three things, constantly conflated

| Term | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Predictive **coding** | a specific neural implementation |
| Predictive **processing** | a framework |
| Free energy principle | a mathematical claim, much wider |

Published work conflates these. Don't.
