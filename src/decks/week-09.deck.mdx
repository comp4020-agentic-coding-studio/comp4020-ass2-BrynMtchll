# Nagel, 1974

---

## What is it like to be a bat?

Four words that have done more work in philosophy of mind than almost any
others.

The claim: there is something it is like, and we have no access to it.

---

## Akins' objection

The question may presuppose something false —

that bat sensory systems are **representational** in the way the question
requires.

---

## Why that matters enormously

| If | Then our failure to answer |
| --- | --- |
| The question is well-formed | tells us about the limits of imagination |
| The question is malformed | tells us about **the question** |
