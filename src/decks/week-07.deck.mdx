# Choice blindness

---

## Johansson et al., 2005

Choose the more attractive of two faces.

Experimenter covertly hands you **the one you rejected**.

Asks why you picked it.

---

## Detection

| Measure | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Manipulations easily spotted | fewer than 1 in 10 |
| Exposed by end of session | under 20% |
| Said in advance they would notice | **84%** |

---

## And then they explained

Citing features specific to the face they had actually turned down.

Fluently. At length. Without hesitation.

# The objection

---

## Petitmengin et al., 2013

Give people room to **examine** their experience rather than pressure to
explain it.

The effect largely dissolves. Recognition sometimes 45 minutes later.

---

## So grade it properly

Strong reading — *introspection is simply unreliable* — **[C]**

Weaker reading is more interesting:

unreliable **under the conditions where we usually deploy it** — while being
asked to account for ourselves.
