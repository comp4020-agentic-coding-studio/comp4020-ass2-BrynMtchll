# The formal shape

---

## Nothing tests alone

Duhem, sharpened by Quine, 1951.

No observation confronts a hypothesis by itself.

*And read Laudan & Leplin against it — our own rules require it.*

---

## Agrippa's trilemma

Any justification is:

1. circular
2. infinite
3. stops somewhere arbitrary

Pick a horn. Defend it in the seminar.

---

## No Free Lunch

Wolpert & Macready, 1997

Averaged over all problems, every learner performs identically.

Any bias that helps somewhere **must hurt elsewhere.**

There is no assumption-free learner. This is a theorem.
