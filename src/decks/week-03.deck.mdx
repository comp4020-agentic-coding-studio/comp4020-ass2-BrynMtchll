## Jennings, on one cell

*Stentor roeseli*, under repeated stimulation:

bend away → alter cilia → contract → detach

A hierarchy of responses. In a single cell.

---

## Then it was discredited

The behaviourist era could not reproduce it.

It stayed discredited for most of a century.

---

Replicated it.

And found why the replications failed.

| Species | Mode of life |
| --- | --- |
| *S. coeruleus* | motile, builds no tube |

Different problem. Different behaviour. Of course.

## Why this case and not a fraud case

No misconduct. No statistical trickery.

A species name nobody checked, and **everyone behaved reasonably.**

This is the failure mode the course is about.
