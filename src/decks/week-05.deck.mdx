# The map, and what it was really for

SLOP3233 · Week 5

Tuyet Nabarro

---

## What association cannot do

Act on a part of the world you are not currently touching.

For that you need something held that is not being sensed.

---

## Tolman, 1948

Rats in mazes doing things their training never rewarded.

Not a chain of associations. Something **map-like**.

---

## O'Keefe & Dostrovsky, 1971

Cells that fire for place.

The story looked finished: there is a spatial system, here are its parts.

Nobel, eventually.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Then 2016

---

## Constantinescu, O'Reilly & Behrens

People navigating a two-dimensional space of **concepts**. Not places.

---

## The signal turned up anyway

Hexagonally symmetric. Entorhinal cortex and vmPFC.

Stable across sessions a week apart.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

If one mechanism organises relational structure generally, space is just the instance we could see.

---

## Which is why we found it there first

Not because space is special.

Because space is **visible** — you can put an animal in a maze and watch.

---

## What this does to the course

This week stops being a step on the road to cognition.

It becomes closer to the **substrate** the later weeks run on.

---

## For the formal version

Whittington et al., 2020 — the Tolman–Eichenbaum Machine.

One model, spatial and relational memory unified. On the reading list, not
examinable.
