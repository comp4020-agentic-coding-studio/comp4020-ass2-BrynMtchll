# How and Why You're Dumb

SLOP3233 · Week 1

Halvard Emms

---

{/* _class: quote */}

You are a finite thing inside a world that is not.

---

## The constraint

A finite system cannot hold a world larger than itself.

It holds a **compression**.

Every compression throws something away.

---

## This is not yet about brains

Ashby, 1956 — *the law of requisite variety*

> A regulator needs at least as much variety as the disturbances it must counter.

You cannot regulate what you cannot represent.

---

## And it gets worse

Conant & Ashby, 1970

Any successful regulator must **contain a model** of what it regulates.

So the model is not optional. Only its accuracy is negotiable.

---

## Shannon gave us the price list

**Rate–distortion theory**

Given a fixed capacity: what is the least damage you can do?

Every organism in this course is answering that question. None of them knows it.
